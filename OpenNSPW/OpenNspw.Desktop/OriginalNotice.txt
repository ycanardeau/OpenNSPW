Naval South Pacific War (NSPW)
==============================

OpenNSPW includes files of the original games, unmodified, so that it plays without them:
NSPW NET's sounds, maps and scenarios, and NSPW 1.22's executable (NSPW_122.exe), one map
and two sounds.

Naval South Pacific War is a game for Windows by Ken-ichi Tokumitsu (徳光 健一) /
Nara-Shika Seisakusho (奈良鹿製作所). All copyrights of these files belong to him.

According to his manuals, the games are freeware, which may generally be redistributed, and
the author asks to be told when they are reposted. They are provided as is: the author
accepts no responsibility for any damage caused by their use.

The author's site: http://narashikabranch.web.fc2.com/

These files are not covered by OpenNSPW's license.
